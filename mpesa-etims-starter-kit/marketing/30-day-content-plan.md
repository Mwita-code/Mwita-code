# 30-day faceless content plan

**Format:** 15–40 second vertical videos. Use a screen recording of the tracker or text on a plain background,
plus a voiceover (your voice, or a free text-to-speech voice in CapCut). Post the same video to TikTok, Instagram Reels,
YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status. Copy the hook as on-screen text in the first 2 seconds.

**Mix:** about 70% teaching (free value), 20% stories and relatable posts, 10% direct offer.
**Call to action (CTA) options:** "Link in bio" · "Comment RECORDS and I'll send it" · "Follow for part 2".

## Facebook group posts (rotate; value first, no links in the post unless the group allows it)

1. **"Free tip:"** "Many of us lose money through credit sales we forget. Write down every 'nitalipa kesho'. If you
   want the simple sheet I use, comment 'SHEET' and I'll inbox you."
2. **Question post:** "Business owners, how do you keep records? Notebook, Excel, an app, or memory? 😅" (Comments
   = leads. Reply helpfully, then DM.)
3. **eTIMS explainer:** Paste the day-2 explanation as text and end with "Confirm details on kra.go.ke".
4. **Checklist post:** The daily checklist from the guide, page 9.

## Weekly review (every Sunday, 15 minutes)

- Which 2 videos got the most views? Make 2 new videos with similar hooks.
- What did people ask in comments or DMs? Turn each question into a video.
- How many sales? Which channel did they come from? (Ask every buyer "Where did you hear about us?")

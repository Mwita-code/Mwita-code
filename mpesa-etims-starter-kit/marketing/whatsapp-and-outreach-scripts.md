# WhatsApp & outreach scripts

Replace [BRACKETS] before sending. Be polite and don't spam: send at most one follow-up to people who don't reply.

## 1. WhatsApp Status (post daily, rotate)

- "Biashara owners 🇰🇪: unajua faida yako halisi ya mwezi huu? 📒 Simple M-Pesa & eTIMS record kit. Reply 'KIT' for details."
- Screenshot of the Monthly Summary + "This is what 10 minutes a day of records looks like 👆"
- "KRA now checks business expenses against eTIMS invoices. Do your suppliers give you one? Reply 'eTIMS' and I'll send a free tip."
- Testimonial screenshot (with permission).

## 2. Broadcast message (twice a week max)

> Habari [NAME]! 👋 I've made a simple **record kit for small businesses**: track M-Pesa and cash sales, expenses,
> people who owe you, and stay ready for KRA eTIMS. Works on your phone. One-time **KSh 999** (launch price
> **KSh 699** this week). Want me to send you a short video of how it works?

## 3. Direct message to a business owner you know

> Hi [NAME], how's business at [SHOP]? Quick question: how do you keep track of sales and M-Pesa payments right
> now? I've been helping small businesses set up simple records (especially with the new eTIMS stuff). If you like,
> I can show you in 5 minutes. No pressure at all 🙏

## 4. Walk-in pitch (local shops, salons, kiosks)

> "Habari! Mimi ni [NAME]. Nasaidia wafanyabiashara kuweka rekodi rahisi za mauzo, M-Pesa na matumizi kwenye simu,
> ili wajue faida yao na wawe tayari kwa eTIMS ya KRA. Naweza kukuonyesha kwa dakika tano?"
>
> (Show the tracker on your phone. Offer the Setup Service at KSh 2,500, where you set it up right there, or the Full Kit at KSh 999.)

## 5. Referral partner message (chama leaders, SACCO officers, trainers, cyber cafés)

> Hi [NAME], I've made a record-keeping kit for small businesses (M-Pesa + eTIMS). Many of your members/customers
> could use it. I'd like to offer you **KSh 300 for every Full Kit sold through you** (30%). I'll give you a
> personal link/code to track it. Can I send you a free copy to look at first?

(On Selar, use affiliate links if available on your plan, or a discount code per partner, to track referrals.)

## 6. Replies to common questions

| They ask | You reply |
|---|---|
| "Is this from KRA?" | "No, it's an independent record-keeping tool and guide. It helps you keep records and understand eTIMS, but you still file with KRA yourself." |
| "Will it file my taxes?" | "No, it keeps your records organised and shows an estimate so you can save. Filing is done on iTax/eCitizen." |
| "I don't have a computer." | "It works on your phone in Google Sheets. I'll show you how, or take the setup package and we do it together." |
| "Is it monthly?" | "No, you pay once and it's yours." |
| "Can you tell me how much tax I owe?" | "I can't give tax advice for your specific case. Please confirm with KRA or a tax professional. The kit helps you have the right records ready." |
| "Too expensive." | "The Basic tracker is KSh 499. One forgotten credit sale usually costs more than that 🙂" |
| "How do I pay?" | "Pay through this link [SELAR LINK] with M-Pesa and the files download instantly." |

## 1b. The app (new)

`/app` in this repo is a full working **Biashara Records app**: big picture buttons, Kiswahili/English, works offline on
any smartphone or computer, and data stays on the phone. See `app/README.md` to put it online for free.

How it fits the business:
- **Free app = audience builder.** Share the link in every TikTok, WhatsApp and Facebook post ("Free app for your
  business records"). Free tools spread much faster than paid ones.
- **Paid offers on top:** the Setup Service (KSh 2,500: install it on their phone, enter their details, train them),
  the Full Kit (guides + Excel tracker for those who prefer Excel), and later group training for chamas and SACCOs.
- Each app screen is also ready-made video content: screen-record it for the 30-day plan.

## 2. The problem we solve

- Kenyan small businesses sell across M-Pesa, cash, WhatsApp and credit, but keep records in notebooks or not at all.
- From 1 Jan 2026, KRA checks income and expenses declared in returns against eTIMS and other data, so
  **expenses without eTIMS invoices may be rejected**. Many owners don't understand eTIMS and are worried about it.
- Accounting software (KSh 400–1,000+/month) is too much for a kiosk, salon or mama mboga.
  A **one-time KSh 500–1,500 kit** is an easy yes.

**Target customers:** shop and kiosk owners, salons and barbers, food vendors, boutique and Instagram sellers,
boda/transport operators, small wholesalers, and new freelancers. Mostly aged 22–45, phone-first, on WhatsApp, TikTok and Facebook.

## 4. Where to sell (Deliver)

1. **Selar** (selar.com): accepts M-Pesa in KSh, free to start, and delivers files automatically after payment. **Use this first.**
2. **Direct M-Pesa** (Till/Pochi) + WhatsApp: customer pays, you send the files. Good for early sales but manual.
   Use a WhatsApp Business catalogue.
3. Later, for buyers outside Kenya: Payhip or Gumroad (they pay out via PayPal or bank, not directly to M-Pesa).

Check each platform's current fees and payout rules before you start.

## 7. Rules that protect you

- **Never give tax rulings.** The kit is "record-keeping help and general education". For specific cases, say:
  "Please confirm with KRA or a tax professional." All tax facts in the guide are kept general and point to kra.go.ke.
- **Re-check eTIMS/tax facts every 3 months** (KRA changes rules often, e.g. Finance Acts in July) and update the guide.
- **Don't use KRA or Safaricom logos** or say you're "official", "approved" or "KRA partner". The disclaimer is already in the files.
- **Pay your own taxes on this income.** Record your sales in your own copy of the tracker.
- Offer a simple refund promise ("If the file doesn't open, we fix it or refund you") to build trust.
